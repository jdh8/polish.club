# Game-forcing 2♣

The 2♣ response is considered two-over-one game-forcing in this bidding system.
It either is natural or conceals a fit in diamonds.

| 1♦ - 2♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♦  | MIN, 11--15, (5+♦ or (41)44)
| 2♥  | S/T, 15--17, 4+♥
| 2♠  | S/T, 15--17, 4+♠
| 2NT | MIN, 11--15, 4441
| 3♣  | S/T, 15+, 4+♣
| 3♦  | S/T, 15+, 6+♦
| 3♥! | SPL, (11--14 or 18--20), 0--1♥, 0--3♠, 4+♣
| 3♠! | SPL, (11--14 or 18--20), 0--1♠, 0--3♥, 4+♣
| 3NT | S/T, 15--17, 4441
