# Natural 1♥

The major suit openings show 11--16 points and 5+ cards.  They can be light in
[late seats](Openings.md#late-seats).  I separate 1♥♠ into two chapters due to
subtle differences in their continuations.

As 1♠ is natural, transfers only serve the heart raise.

| 1♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♠   | F, 4+♠
| 1NT  | NAT NF, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♣!  | (R), FG, 0--3♥, 0--3♠
| 2♦!  | TRF, CONST 3+♥
| 2♥   | PRE, 3+♥
| 2♠   | PRE, 6+♠
| 2NT! | LIM+, 4+♥
| 3♣♦! | INV+, 5+#, 4+♥
| 3♥   | PRE, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, 0--1♠, 4+♥
| 3NT! | COG, 33(43)
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♥

## By passed hand

A passed hand cannot force to game, so 2♣ becomes [Drury](1H/Drury.md).  Other
responses are unchanged.

A light opener holds 3+♠ and passes 1♠ or 1NT, so opener's rebids over them
still show 11+ points.

| P - 1♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♣! | LIM, 3+♥
