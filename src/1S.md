# Natural 1♠

The major suit openings show 11--16 points and 5+ cards.  I separate them into
two chapters due to subtle differences in their continuations.

To allow a choice between 4♥ and 4♠, 1♠-3♥ shows an invitation with 36(xx)
rather than 45(xx).

| 1♠-  | 11--16, 5+♠ |
|------|-------------|
| 1NT  | NAT NF, 0--3♠
| 2♣!  | (R), FG, 0--3♠, 0--4♥
| 2♦!  | TRF, INV+ 5+♥ or CONST 6+♥
| 2♥!  | TRF, CONST 3+♠
| 2♠!  | PRE, 3+♠
| 2NT! | LIM+, 4+♠
| 3♣♦! | INV+, 5+#, 4+♠
| 3♥!  | INV, 6+♥, 3=♠
| 3♠!  | PRE, 4+♠
| 3NT! | COG, 33(43)
| 4X!  | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♠
| 4♠   | PRE, UNBAL 4+♠
