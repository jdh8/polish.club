# Natural 1♠

The major suit openings show 11--16 points and 5+ cards.  They can be light in
[late seats](Openings.md#late-seats).  I separate 1♥♠ into two chapters due to
subtle differences in their continuations.

[Ambra] 2♦ already handles invitation with both majors.  Better to repurpose 3♥
for preemptive hands.

[Ambra]: http://www.infobridge.it/Sistemi_ambra_eng.pdf

| 2♣!   | (R), FG, 0--3♠, 0--4♥
| 2♦!   | TRF: (INV+, 5+♥) or (CONST, 6+♥)
| 2♥!   | TRF, CONST 3+♠

| 4♣♦♥! | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♠

## By passed hand

A passed hand cannot force to game, so 2♣ becomes [Drury](1S/Drury.md).  Other
responses are unchanged.

A light opener passes 1NT, so opener's rebids over it still show 11+ points.
