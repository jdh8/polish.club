# Competing at the 1-level

*Polish Club 2020* already incorporates Transfer Walsh at the 1-level.  One
obvious advantage is that we get a "support double" by completing the transfer
even without intervention. I generalize it to all non-jump responses
to end the debate between standard and negative free bids.

- 1♦ or (1♦)X: transfer, 4+♥
- 1♥ or (1♥)X: transfer, 4+♠
- 1♠: transfer to 1NT, partly ask for stopper
- 2♣: transfer, 5+♦
- 2♠: transfer, 5+♣

We somehow ignore the meaning of overcalls to deal with artificial and psychic
bids.  If we need to keep the bidding open, the nebulous 1♠ gets the job done.

This competitive Transfer Walsh is the **Cachalot** (Bessis, *Le Bridgeur*
852, 2011; Voldoire, 909, 2017).  Opener completes a major transfer at the
1-level with exactly 3-card support, right-siding the suit so the intervenor
leads.  With four trumps opener supports at the 2-level, minimum, and goes
higher with extras; a balanced minimum without the fit bids 1NT, promising a
stopper opposite an overcall.  Above the completion, opener continues as over
the uncontested [positive 1M](1M.md), where Odwrotka 2♦, the natural raises,
and the side suits all keep their meaning.  Because Odwrotka still shows the
game-forcing 3-card raise, the bare completion is limited to 11--17, though
wider than the minimum 2M.  The full rebid tables follow in
[opener's rebids](COMP_1/Opener.md).

## Over (X)

Natural notrump is not very useful after a double, so let's also use it for
transfers.  Then 1♠ takes care of diamonds, the only suit left over.

| 1♣(X) | - |
|-------|---|
| XX    | NF BAL G/T, 10+
| 1♦!   | TRF, 7+, 4+♥
| 1♥!   | TRF, 7+, 4+♠
| 1♠!   | TRF, 7+, 5+♦
| 1NT!  | TRF, 7+, 5+♣
| 2♣    | PRE, 0--7, 5+♣
| 2X    | PRE, 0--7, 6+#
| 2NT!  | TRF, (PRE 7+♣ or FG 6+♣)
| 3♣!   | TRF, (PRE 7+♦ or FG 6+♦)
| 3♦!   | TRF, (PRE 7+♥ or FG 6+♥)
| 3♥!   | TRF, (PRE 7+♠ or FG 6+♠)
| 3♠!   | Gambling, SOL 7+ suit with no stopper outside
| 3NT!  | Gambling, SOL 7+ suit with one stopper outside

After 1♦! and 1♥!, opener completes the major and continues as over
[positive 1M](1M.md); lacking an overcall, the full structure, Odwrotka 2♦
included, is available.  Over the minor transfers 1♠! and 1NT!, opener bids the
minor with a fit, 1NT with a stopper, or a natural new suit.  Opener completes
the two-way 2NT!--3♥! transfers, after which the weak hand passes and the strong
hand drives to game; 3♠!/3NT! are P/C.

## Over (1♦)

Transferring to diamonds is not very useful, so 2♣ falls back to a single raise.

| 1♣(1♦) | - |
|--------|---|
| X!     | TRF, 7+, 4+♥
| 1♥!    | TRF, 7+, 4+♠
| 1♠!    | TRF to 1NT, 8+
| 1NT    | NAT, 8--10
| 2♣     | NF, 5--10, 5+♣
| 2♦!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♥
| 2♠!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♣
| 2NT    | NAT INV, 10--11
| 3♣     | PRE, 6+♣
| 3♦!    | TRF, (PRE 7+♥ or FG 6+♥)
| 3♥!    | TRF, (PRE 7+♠ or FG 6+♠)
| 3♠!    | Gambling, SOL 7+ suit without stopper

After X! and 1♥!, opener completes and continues as over [positive 1M](1M.md),
the rebid of 2♦ being the usual Odwrotka.  Over 1♠!, opener bids 1NT with a
diamond stopper, else a suit.  The transfers 2♦! and 2♠! are completed in the
shown suit, broken with extras; 3♦!/3♥! and the gambling 3♠! are P/C.

## Over (1♥)

Transferring to hearts is not very useful, so 2♦ falls back to a negative free
bid.

| 1♣(1♥) | - |
|--------|---|
| X!     | TRF, 7+, 4+♠
| 1♠!    | TRF to 1NT, 8+
| 1NT    | NAT, 8--10
| 2♣!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♦
| 2♦     | NF, 5--10, 6+♦
| 2♠!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♣
| 2NT    | NAT INV, 10--11
| 3♣     | PRE, 6+♣
| 3♦     | PRE, 7+♦
| 3♥!    | TRF, (PRE 7+♠ or FG 6+♠)
| 3♠!    | Gambling, SOL 7+ suit without stopper

After X!, opener completes 1♠ and continues as over [positive 1M](1M.md), but 2♥
is a cuebid, a strong spade raise, since opener instead passes X! to defend with
real hearts.  Over 1♠!, opener bids 1NT with a heart stopper, else a suit.  The
transfers 2♣! and 2♠! are completed, broken with extras; 3♥! and the gambling
3♠! are P/C.

## Over (1♠)

Transferring to spades is not very useful, so 2♥ falls back to a negative free
bid.

| 1♣(1♠) | - |
|--------|---|
| X!     | NEG, 7+, (4--5♥ or BAL FG)
| 1NT    | NAT, 8--10
| 2♣!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♦
| 2♦!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♥
| 2♥     | NF, 5--10, usually 6+♥
| 2♠!    | TRF, 10+, 5+♣
| 2NT    | NAT INV, 10--11
| 3♣     | PRE, 6+♣
| 3♦♥    | PRE, 7+#
| 3♠!    | Gambling, SOL 7+ suit without stopper

Here X! is negative rather than a transfer: opener raises hearts with four, bids
1NT with a spade stopper, or bids naturally.  The transfers 2♣!, 2♦!, and 2♠!
are completed in the shown suit and continue as the matching uncontested
auction.  The gambling 3♠! is P/C.
