## Minor suit cuebid

Michaels is the de facto standard [two-suited overcall][two], and we use it
against every natural opening, even a minor suit that may be as short as one
card.  Showing both majors keeps the auction low and secures our heart fit.

[two]: https://www.blakjak.org/two_suit.htm

Note that natural openings are protected from brown stickers.  Only over an
artificial opening can we cue with 5-5 including opponents' nominal suit.

| (1m)     |   Overcall  | Meaning |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| NAT, 1+# | Michaels 2m | 5+♠, 5+♥
| ART, 0+# | Wilkosz  2♦ | 5+♠♥, 5+ another suit

## P P P (1y)

By passed hand, overcalls that require opening strength (7.5&minus; NLTC) become
one trick weaker.  1NT becomes minor suit oriented takeout.

| P P P (1y) | (Quasi-)NAT, x < y |
|------|---|
| X    | T/O, 8.5&minus; NLTC
| 1NT! | T/O, 8.5&minus; NLTC, 4+ 4+ in the lowest two unbid suits
| 2x   | 8.5&minus; NLTC, 5+#

## (1y) -

Balancing overcalls tend to be intermediate.  Jump overcalls are sound because
there is no one to preempt.  On the other hand, constructive overcalls are light
because partner may have passed a weak notrump.

An unpassed 2NT is no longer unusual because we've got more steps on the notrump
ladder.

- 13--15 HCP: 1NT
- 16--18 HCP: X, then notrump
- 19--21 HCP: 2NT

| (1y) - - | x < y < z |
|--------|---|
| X      | T/O, 8+ HCP
| 1z     | 8--15 HCP, 4+#
| 1NT    | 13--15 HCP with stopper
| 2x     | 8--15 HCP, 5+#
| 2z, 3x | 11--15 HCP, 6+#
| 2NT    | 19--21 HCP with stopper
