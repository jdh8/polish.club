# Semi-forcing 1NT

Opener passes 1♥ - 1NT only with a balanced 11--12.  The best major fit at
1♥ - 1NT - - is balanced 5-3.  With 13--14, opener has to bid, so 2♣ can be a
doubleton as in [1♠ - 1NT](../1S/1NT.md), and 2♦ can be 4531.

| 1♥ - 1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣   | 11--16, 2+♣
| 2♦   | 11--16, 3+♦
| 2♥   | 11--14, 6+♥
| 2♠   | 15--16, 4+♠
| 2NT! | 15--16, 6=♥, ≤ ♥AJ9
| 3♣♦  | 15--16, 5+#
| 3♥   | 15--16, ((6+♥, AJT+) or 7+♥)
| 3♠   | 15--16, 4+♠, 6+♥
| 3NT! | Gambling, 7+♥ AKQ, no outside A/K

Just like [1♠ - 1NT](../1S/1NT.md), the threshold of suit quality is based on
[Binky Points][binky].

[binky]: https://bridge.thomasoandrews.com/bridge/valuations/additive.html

## Impossible spades

Spades that responder rebids must be artificial because 1NT already denies
spades.  Following [Kokish--Kraft][kk], the impossible 2♠ over 2♣♦ is forcing and
gathers the invitations that need opener's help to judge: a notrump invitation
holding a fit for opener's minor, an invitational raise of that minor, and a
3-card limit raise of hearts with a shortage that would rather not pass 3♥ to
show it.  Over 2♦ it also carries an invitation on long clubs.  The direct bids
are the rest by fast arrival: 2NT denies a fit for the minor, the raise of the
minor is a courtesy raise, 3♦ over 2♣ is an invitation on a good suit, and 3♥
is the balanced limit raise.

[kk]: https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WEAK-NOTRUMP-SYSTEM-Kokish-Kraft-Jan-2008.pdf

Opener answers 2♠ with reject advances, naming the invitations it declines.
2NT would pass any invitation; each step up accepts more, and the three-level
suits also show the shape behind the acceptance.  Responder then places the
contract.  The limit raise with a shortage signs off in 3♥ over 2NT and bids
game — or shows the shortage — once opener bids higher.

Over 2♥ opener has shown the hand, so the impossible 2♠ changes job: it asks
which minor opener prefers, letting a weak hand escape the 6--1 heart fit into
its long minor, or into the better minor with both.  Opener's 2NT prefers
diamonds and 3♣ prefers clubs; responder passes or corrects, or bids 3♥ to
raise after all — the slow route marks concentrated spade values.  The direct
raises are sorted by where responder's values lie, and 3♠ is the limit raise
with a shortage.

## Precision 2NT

Like [1♠ - 1NT - 2NT](../1S/1NT.md#precision-2nt), opener has pinned a narrow
hand — a maximum with a weak six-card suit — so responder simply places the
contract.  Here the spade puppet has nothing to find: 1NT denied spades, so
there is no second major, and the escape suits ♣♦ straddle hearts rather than
sitting below the trump suit. Every call is therefore a natural sign-off — pass
with a balanced minimum or retreat to a long minor or take the 6--3 heart fit at
the level your strength buys.

| 3♦  | S/O, 6+♦
| 3♥  | S/O, 3=♥
| 3NT | S/O, 0--2♥
| 4♥  | S/O, 3=♥
