# Transfer 2♦

This 2♦ transfer from [Ambra](http://www.infobridge.it/Sistemi_ambra_eng.pdf)
makes it easier for opener to judge the right contract.

- Invitational+ (10+) 5+♥
- Constructive (8--10) 6+♥

In standard bidding methods, 1♠-1NT may conceal a heart fit.  Finding the right
contract for a 3-5 heart fit is challenging.  Bart-style gadgets try to fix it
after a whole round of bidding.

| 1♠-2♦- | TRF, 5+♥ |
|--------|----------|
| 2♥     | NF, 2--4♥
| 2♠     | NF, 0--2♥
| 2NT!   | FG, 2+♥
| 3♣♦    | FG, 4+#, 0--1♥
| 3♥     | INV, 3--4♥
| 3♠     | FG, 6+♠, 0--1♥
| 3NT!   | MIN SPL, 0--1♦, 4=♥
| 4♣!    | MIN SPL, 0--1♣, 4=♥
| 4♦!    | MIN SPL, 0--1♦, 5+♥
| 4♥!    | MIN SPL, 0--1♣, 5+♥
