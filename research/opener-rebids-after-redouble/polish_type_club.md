# Opener's continuations after 1♣ (X) XX in Polish-type club systems

Checked 2026-11-10. Builds on `research/opener-1d-after-redouble.md` (local file in the
repo; its 2,310-card sweep, BBO Full Disclosure sweep and ~95 searches are not repeated
here). New work in this pass: full-text reads of the redouble chapters of WJ2000
(English), WJ 2005 (Polish book and Neill's English web translation), WJ05 short
descriptions (two copies), WJ DUCE Q1.2021, Fools and Stancescu–Draghicescu notes, and
the convention cards of Krawczyk–Szymański, Sucharda–Andruszkiewicz,
Bocheński–Cichocki, Bizon–Blat, Balicki–Żmudziński (2004) and Chmurski–Gawryś (2007).
Five web searches (Polish, English, Swedish).

Labels: **specific** = the source names an auction after our XX and gives opener's
meaning; **generic** = a blanket rule ("after XX the auction is forcing", "later doubles
are penalty") that reaches 1♣ (X) XX only by application. **Polish-type** = multi-way 1♣
with a weak balanced range; **unbal-♦** = 1♣ balanced-or-clubs with 1♦ unbalanced
(included as comparators, not Polish-type).

## Source-by-source catalogue (objective)

### Takeaway
Only two sources say anything concrete about opener's own calls after a values XX:
Jassem's WJ texts (strength rule, plus a pass-then-pull example) and the Romanian
Stancescu–Draghicescu notes (pass = min bal, Dbl = 18–20 bal). Three Polish cards state
only that the auction is forcing after XX. Nobody writes a 1♣-specific table.

### Cited Findings

**2. WJ 2005 — D. J. Neill's English web translation (same content)**
- XX: "A redouble shows a hand with 10+ HCP. The goals are both to catch the enemy doubled, and to find the best contract." — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)
- "Openers immediate bid below 2 of the opening suit over fourth-seats bid shows a minimum opening: a) 1♠ (X) XX 2♣ / 2♦ Pass ? b) 1♠ (X) XX 2♣ / Pass Pass X Pass / 2♦. - "Other situations concern when the auction has risen past the level of 2 of openers suit, e.g.: 1♠ (X) XX (2♥) 3♣ Pass ? Openers bids indicate a good hand — he went above the safety level: 2♠ ..." — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)

- The WJ family (items 1–4) is one author's rule restated; it counts as one source, not four.
- In WJ's example (b), opener passes, partner's double is penalty, and opener's pull shows "more(!) strength". That implies opener's pass over the overcall is forcing, and that pass-then-pull is the stronger route. Krawczyk–Szymański's "The strongest bid is pass and then taking out the partner's double" matches this, but that line sits in a general box and is not tied to XX.

## After 1♣ (X) XX (bid): is opener's pass forcing? Is opener's double penalty?

### Takeaway
The forcing pass is well supported: WJ2000 says the auction is forcing after XX, and three Polish cards print forcing limits ("forces to our double or 1NT or 2♣/♦"; "forced to 2NT"). No Polish-type source defines opener's direct double. The only definition is Stancescu–Draghicescu's "Dbl 18-20 bal". Fools's "X : 4+ trumps" is penalty, but Fools is not Polish-type.

### Cited Findings
- Forcing (generic): "Over partner's redouble the auction is forcing (over the enemy we are required to bid one more time)." — [WJ2000 p. 34](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2000.pdf)
- Forcing limit, 1♣-specific: "1♣/♦-(X)-XX forces to our double or 1NT or 2♣/♦" — [Krawczyk–Szymański](https://wyniki.pzbs.pl/2020/wuc/web/CC/CC%20POL%202%20Krawczyk-Szymanski.pdf)
- Forcing limit, generic: "All sequences when we use strong redouble are forced to 2NT" — [Sucharda–Andruszkiewicz](https://wyniki.pzbs.pl/2020/wuc/web/CC/CC%20POL%203%20Sucharda-Andruszkiewicz.pdf)
- Implicit forcing: "it's our hand, any later bid is nat RF" — [Stancescu–Draghicescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf)
- Meaning of pass: "pass showing min bal" (Stancescu); "pass : F w/mostly 2-3 trumps" (Fools); WJ's pass is followed by partner's double and a pull that shows "more(!) strength" — [Stancescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf); [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md); [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)
- Meaning of double: "Dbl 18-20 bal" (Stancescu, a strength-showing balanced double, not labelled penalty); "X : 4+ trumps" (Fools); "All later doubles are for penalty" (Fools, generic) — [Stancescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf); [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md)
- Partner's double after opener's pass is penalty in WJ: opener "is not happy defending 2♣-X" when he pulls it. — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)
- Cheap bids: "Openers immediate bid below 2 of the opening suit over fourth-seats bid shows a minimum opening"; bids past two of the opening suit "indicate a good hand". Shape: "good distribution (5-5) or shortness in clubs" (in the 2♣-overcall example). — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)
- NT: Fools "NT : nat, nF w/stopper" (by either player). No Polish-type source gives opener's NT. — [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md)
- Stancescu–Draghicescu's 1♣ has a balanced range of "11-14 or 18-20", so "Dbl 18-20 bal" is opener's strong balanced range. — [Stancescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf)

### Inferences
- "1♣-(X)-XX forces to our double or 1NT or 2♣/♦" is a floor: below it, a pass by either partner is forcing, and a double ends the obligation. That double is our penalty double. The card does not say which partner doubles.
- On Stancescu's wording, "After pass/rebl" seems to mean after responder's pass or XX when fourth hand bids. After 1♣ (X) XX (P) opener has nothing to double, so "Dbl 18-20 bal" can only apply when fourth hand bids. This reading is mine; the notes give no example.
- No Polish-type source endorses the book's "Double: penalty" for opener directly. Fools does ("4+ trumps"), and so do the generic "later doubles penalty" rules. Stancescu points the other way, with a double that describes strength and shape.

### Gaps
- No Polish-type source says what opener's direct double shows after 1♣ (X) XX (bid).
- No source states the trump length for opener's pass vs. double (except Fools's "2-3" vs. "4+").

## After 1♣ (X) XX (P): weak balanced vs. strong vs. clubs

### Takeaway
Only two sources address this branch, and neither is WJ. Stancescu–Draghicescu say "opener bids naturally" with "full system on". Fools splits pass (GF or weak balanced), new suit, a 6+ suit rebid at minimum, and jumps. The WJ strength rule reaches it only through the short description's "przed partnerem" wording.

### Cited Findings
- "After pass/rebl, opener bids naturally ... Follow-up bidding full system on (including double checkback) unless opponents overcall with a suit" — [Stancescu–Draghicescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf)
- "pass : GF any (but not very offensive) or weak bal"; "new suit : 5+ 5(4)+, offensive or min"; "rebid suit : 6+suit, min"; "jumps : semiPRE w/minimal strength and freakish distributions" — [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md)
- WJ: "Jeśli po rekontrze otwierający licytuje przed partnerem, to: poniżej dwóch w kolor otwarcia licytacja wskazuje dół otwarcia, powyżej dwóch w kolor otwarcia – nadwyżki." — [WJ05StrPol](https://stara.pzbs.pl/ksiazki/inni_wydawcy/WJ05StrPol.pdf). Jassem's English: "shows a minimum if the bid is cheaper than two in the opening bid, but shows extras otherwise." — [jassem.pl 71](https://web.archive.org/web/20160415133312/http://jassem.pl/articles/71.pdf)
- WJ weak variant: "w drugim okrążeniu licytacji otwierający nie licytuje trefli – również w licytacji dwustronnej" (prior survey) — [WJ05StrPol](https://stara.pzbs.pl/ksiazki/inni_wydawcy/WJ05StrPol.pdf)
- Transfer-XX systems use pass or the completion differently: Sublime's "1NT: 2–3 card support, weak NT with good stop"; WJ XXI plus's "pas otwierającego na rekontrę wskazuje na siłę 15+". — [Sublime](https://sublime.is/bidding-system.html); [bridgeok](http://bridgeok.cba.pl/00%20WJ%20XXI%20plus%20Transfery%20po%20interwencji.html)
- In the prior 2,310-card and 115-system Full Disclosure sweep, Stancescu–Draghicescu were the only card or note found stating opener's action after 1♣ (X) XX. — `research/opener-1d-after-redouble.md` (local, Sweep coverage)

### Inferences
- With weak balanced hands, the sources split. Stancescu's "pass showing min bal" (if it covers (P)) and Fools's "weak bal" both put the hand on pass. The WJ strength rule would put it on a cheap bid; no WJ text names pass or 1NT here.
- With a strong hand: Fools's pass also covers "GF any", WJ bids at or above two of the opening suit, and Stancescu bids naturally. With clubs, Fools rebids a 6+ suit at minimum, while WJ's weak variant does not rebid clubs and WJ's 2♣ falls on the "extras" side of the rule.

### Gaps
- No Polish-type source gives opener's 1NT, 2NT or 3NT after 1♣ (X) XX (P).
- No Polish-type source says whether opener's pass after (P) is to play (1♣XX) or forcing.

## Forcing-pass catchall, or cuebids as two-suited takeout?

### Takeaway
No source calls opener's pass a "catchall takeout", and none defines a cuebid by opener after 1♣ (X) XX (bid) as two-suited. The nearest is the pass-then-pull pattern (WJ; Krawczyk–Szymański "strongest bid"), in which a forcing pass is followed by pulling partner's penalty double.

### Cited Findings
- "The strongest bid is pass and then taking out the partner's double" — [Krawczyk–Szymański](https://wyniki.pzbs.pl/2020/wuc/web/CC/CC%20POL%202%20Krawczyk-Szymanski.pdf) (general box, not tied to XX)
- WJ sequence b): pass, partner doubles, opener pulls = "not happy defending 2♣-X ... good distribution (5-5) or shortness in clubs ... a stronger(!) hand" — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)
- Fools's pass is "F w/mostly 2-3 trumps", defined by trump length rather than as takeout. The only cuebid-like call it gives is responder's "OP suit by RE : INV w/3-card support". — [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md)
- WJ: a bid past two of the opening suit (e.g. "1♠ (X) XX (2♥) 3♣") shows "a good hand". Cuebids are not mentioned. — [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm)

### Inferences
- The book's "Forcing pass: catchall takeout" is close to the WJ and Krawczyk pass-then-act route. The difference is that those sources expect partner's next double to be penalty, and opener's pull of it to be the strong or shapely action. They do not treat pass as a takeout call.

### Gaps
- No source for "Cuebid: game-forcing two-suited takeout, clubs and a new suit", "3NT: 15–17, to play" or "Other: 18+, natural" after 1♣ (X) XX.

## Generic vs. specific

### Takeaway
Almost everything is generic. Only Krawczyk–Szymański (forcing limit) and Stancescu–Draghicescu (pass, Dbl, natural bids) name 1♣ (X) XX. WJ's rule is generic and illustrated only with 1♠. Fools is generic for any one-level XX.

### Cited Findings
- Specific to 1♣ (X) XX: [Krawczyk–Szymański](https://wyniki.pzbs.pl/2020/wuc/web/CC/CC%20POL%202%20Krawczyk-Szymanski.pdf) ("1♣/♦-(X)-XX forces to ..."); [Stancescu–Draghicescu](https://r.pzbs.pl/2024/tsc/web/CC/CC%20ROU%20Stancescu-Draghicescu_notes.pdf) ("After 1♣DOUBLE ...").
- Generic: [WJ2000](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2000.pdf), [WJ 2005 book](https://e-sochaczew.pl/static/files/partner_docs/500/wspolny_jezyk_2005.pdf), [Neill WJ05](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/WJ2005webpage.htm), [WJ05StrPol](https://stara.pzbs.pl/ksiazki/inni_wydawcy/WJ05StrPol.pdf), [Sucharda–Andruszkiewicz](https://wyniki.pzbs.pl/2020/wuc/web/CC/CC%20POL%203%20Sucharda-Andruszkiewicz.pdf), [Bocheński–Cichocki](https://championships.eurobridge.org/2025EFOTSC/Systems/BOCHENSKI-BOCHENSKI-CICHOCKI.pdf), [Fools](https://github.com/zdenecek/fools/blob/main/sis/doubles_and_redoubles.md), [szkoleniabrydzowe.pl](https://szkoleniabrydzowe.pl/rekontra-bron-obosieczna/).
- XX defined, opener silent: [WJ DUCE](https://iccs.pl/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Wspolny-Jezyk-DUCE-wersja-Q1.2021.pdf), [Balicki–Żmudziński](https://stara.pzbs.pl/kadra/2004/k_konw/balicki_zmudzinski.pdf), [Chmurski–Gawryś](https://stara.pzbs.pl/kadra/2007/open/supermecz3/cc/Chmurski_Gawrys.pdf), plus about 40 cards listed in `research/transfer-walsh-redouble.md`.

### Inferences
- The evidence base is thin and correlated: one author family (WJ), three Polish cards that print only forcing limits, and two non-Polish-type comparators.

### Gaps
- Defensive passages about passes over the opponents' redouble ("Pas po rekontrze jest karny ze strony gracza siedzącego za kontrowanym kolorem", WJ 2005 book, defensive-bidding chapter) concern a different auction and were excluded.
