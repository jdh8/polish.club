# Natural 1♥

The major suit openings show 11--16 points and 5+ cards.  I separate them into
two chapters due to subtle differences in their continuations.

As 1♠ is natural, transfers only serve the heart raise.  The 2♠ response is a
limit raise with a 6-card side suit to allow a choice between 4♥ and 4♠.

| 1♥-  | 11--16, 5+♥ |
|------|-------------|
| 1♠   | F, 4+♠
| 1NT  | NAT NF, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♣!  | (R), FG, 0--3♥, 0--3♠
| 2♦!  | TRF, CONST 3+♥
| 2♥!  | PRE, 3+♥
| 2♠!  | LIM, 3=♥, 6+♠♦♣
| 2NT! | LIM+, 4+♥
| 3♣♦! | INV+, 5+#, 4+♥
| 3♥!  | PRE, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, 0--1♠, 4+♥
| 3NT! | COG, 33(43)
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♥
| 4♥!  | PRE, UNBAL 4+♥

## 1♥-2♠

The heavy-tailed limit raise promises 3-card support and a 6-card side suit.
This agreement is especially useful for spades.  Choice between 4♥♠ arises from
63(xx) but not 54(xx).

| 1♥-2♠- | LIM, 3=♥, 6+X |
|--------|---------------|
| 2NT!   | Invite 6+♠
| 3♣     | Invite 6+♣
| 3♦     | Invite 6+♦
| 3♥     | S/O
