# Natural 1♦ - 1M

The responses 1♦ - 1♥♠ show 4+ cards just like any 5-card major system.  Responder
has 6+ points most of the time, but sometimes responder bids with a freaky hand,
especially with a long major suit and short diamonds.

## Artificial reverse 3♣

It has always been a headache for a minor suit opener to show invitational+
3-card support in 5-card major bidding systems.  This 3♣ convention solves that
problem by replacing the strong jump shift.  This reverse shares spirit with
Odwrotka despite not in standard Polish Club.

This convention is as safe as 3♦.  Opener has 6+♦ and denies all side suits when
merely invitational.

| 1♦ - 1M -<br>3♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♦  | MIN, 4=M
| 3M  | MIN, 5+M
| 3NT | S/O, 4=M
| 4♣! | S/T in M, CTRL
| 4♦  | S/T in ♦
| 4M  | S/O, 5+M

| 1♦ - 1♥ -<br>3♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♠! | S/T in ♥, CTRL

Conventional 1♦ - 2♥♠ already handle non-forcing (4+♥, 5+♠) hands.  Thus,
1♦ - 1♠ - 3♣ - 3♥ must bundle enough values for slam exploration.

| 1♦ - 1♠ -<br>3♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♥  | S/T, 4+♥, 5+♠
| 4♥! | S/T in ♠, CTRL
