# Semi-forcing 1NT

Opener passes 1♥ - 1NT only with a balanced 11--12.  The best major fit at
1♥ - 1NT - - is balanced 5-3.  With 13--14, opener has to bid, so 2♣ can be a
doubleton as in [1♠ - 1NT](../1S/1NT.md), and 2♦ can be 4531.

| 1♥ - 1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣   | 11--16, 2+♣
| 2♦   | 11--16, 3+♦
| 2♥   | 11--14, 6+♥
| 2♠   | 15--16, 4+♠
| 2NT! | 15--16, 6=♥, ≤ ♥AJ9
| 3♣♦  | 15--16, 5+#
| 3♥   | 15--16, ((6+♥, AJT+) or 7+♥)
| 3♠   | 15--16, 4+♠, 6+♥
| 3NT! | Gambling, 7+♥ AKQ, no outside A/K

Just like [1♠ - 1NT](../1S/1NT.md), the threshold of suit quality is based on
[Binky Points][binky].

[binky]: https://bridge.thomasoandrews.com/bridge/valuations/additive.html

## Impossible spades

Spades that responder rebids must be artificial because 1NT already denies
spades.  Over 2♣ the impossible 2♠ is the traditional invitational club raise:
opener signs off in 2NT or 3♣ or bids game.

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♦  | NF, 5+♦
| 2♥  | NF, 2--3♥
| 2♠! | INV, 4+♣
| 2NT | NAT INV
| 3♣  | NF, 5+♣
| 3♦  | INV, 6+♦
| 3♥  | LIM, 3=♥

Over 2♦♥ there are two or more invitations to carry, so 2♠ becomes a
[puppet to 2NT][heitzman].  The following 3-bid is then the invitational version
of the same call, while the direct 3-bid is to play.

[heitzman]: https://bridgewinners.com/article/view/the-impossible-two-spades/

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥  | NF, 1--3♥
| 2♠! | ART INV, PUP
| 2NT | NAT INV
| 3♣  | NF, 6+♣
| 3♦  | NF, 4+♦
| 3♥  | LIM, 3=♥

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2♦ - 2♠! -<br>2NT - | |
|----|-|
| 3♣ | INV, 6+♣
| 3♦ | INV, 4+♦

Over 2♥ opener has shown 6+♥, so the direct minor bids are signoffs out of the
misfit.  The puppet carries the limit raise and invitations in either minor. The
direct 3♥ is the weaker raise with 3 trumps.

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | ART INV, PUP
| 2NT | NAT INV
| 3♣♦ | S/O, 6+#
| 3♥  | NF, 3+♥

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2♥ - 2♠! -<br>2NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣♦ | INV, 6+#
| 3♥  | INV, 2+♥

## Precision 2NT

Like [1♠ - 1NT - 2NT](../1S/1NT.md#precision-2nt), opener has pinned a narrow
hand — a maximum with a weak six-card suit — so responder simply places the
contract.  Here the spade puppet has nothing to find: 1NT denied spades, so
there is no second major, and the escape suits ♣♦ straddle hearts rather than
sitting below the trump suit. Every call is therefore a natural sign-off — pass
with a balanced minimum or retreat to a long minor or take the 6--3 heart fit at
the level your strength buys.

| 1♥ - 1NT -<br>2NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣  | S/O, 6+♣
| 3♦  | S/O, 6+♦
| 3♥  | S/O, 3=♥
| 3NT | S/O, 0--2♥
| 4♥  | S/O, 3=♥
