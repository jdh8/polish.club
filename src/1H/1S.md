# Natural 1♥ - 1♠

As the opening is limited to 11--16, the reverses are not forcing.  The reverses
show a maximum 5+ 5+ two suiter.

By passed hand, a [light opener](../Openings.md#late-seats) passes 1♠ with the
promised 3+♠.  The rebids below are unchanged.

| 1♥ - 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT  | BAL 11--14
| 2♣   | 11--16, 4+♣
| 2♦   | 11--16, 4+♦
| 2♥   | 11--14, 6+♥
| 2♠   | 11--14, 4+♠
| 2NT! | 15--16, 6=♥, ≤ ♥AJ9
| 3♣♦  | 15--16, 5+#
| 3♥   | 15--16, ((6+♥, AJT+) or 7+♥)
| 3♠   | 15--16, 4+♠
| 3NT! | Gambling, 7+♥ AKQ, no outside A/K
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♠
