# Drury 2♣

A passed hand cannot force to game, so 2♣ shows a limit raise instead of the
[game-forcing relay](2C.md).  Other responses to [1♠](../1S.md) are unchanged.

Reverse Drury is de facto standard.  The original Drury becomes the *lesser*
panda.

The 2♦ relay shows a full opening and may contain 4+♥.  Opposite it, responder
can probe hearts with invitational 2♥ and game-forcing 3♥.

| P - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | (R)
| 2♥   | NF, light 4+♥
| 2♠   | PRE, a light opening
| 2NT+ | NAT FG

| P - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - 2♦ - | |
|----|-|
| 2♥ | INV, 4+♥
| 3♥ | FG, 4+♥
