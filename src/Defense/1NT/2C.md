# Landy advances

Partner's Landy 2♣ shows both majors (4+♠ 4+♥, 10--15; 5--11 by a passed hand).
Unlike [Ekren](../../2C.md), Landy is not a preempt, so there is no relay to
discover strength — the advancer places the contract directly.  Weak
hands take a preference, invitational hands jump to 3M, and game hands bid 4M or
offer a COG with 4♣.

| (1NT)2♣- | 4+♠, 4+♥ |
|----------|----------|
| 2♦!      | (R), ♥ = ♠
| 2♥       | COMP, ♥ > ♠
| 2♠       | COMP, ♠ > ♥
| 2NT!     | TRF, 6+♦
| 3♣       | NAT F
| 3♦       | NAT F
| 3♥♠      | INV, 4+#
| 4♣!      | COG
| 4♥♠      | S/O

The 2♦ relay holds no diamonds of its own — the 2♣ bidder cannot pass and names
the better major to play.

| (1NT)2♣-2♦- | (R), ♥ = ♠ |
|-------------|------------|
| 2♥          | ♥ ≥ ♠
| 2♠          | ♠ > ♥

The COG transfers right-side the major so the overcaller declares and the lead
runs up to the concealed advancing hand.

| (1NT)2♣-4♣- | COG |
|-------------|-----|
| 4♦!         | TRF, ♥ ≥ ♠
| 4♥!         | TRF, ♠ > ♥
